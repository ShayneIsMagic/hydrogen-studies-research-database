# Comprehensive Topics Extraction - Spreadsheet Format

## 🎯 Overview

This extraction follows the **exact format** of the Google Sheets spreadsheet to ensure thorough and consistent data collection for all 199+ health topics.

## 📊 Spreadsheet Format Structure

The extraction follows this exact column structure from the Google Sheets document:

| Column | Field Name | Description | Example |
|--------|------------|-------------|---------|
| 1 | **Topic** | Health condition or disease | "Acne" |
| 2 | **Study Title** | Full title of the research study | "Hydrogen Water Treatment for Acne Vulgaris" |
| 3 | **Authors** | Study authors and researchers | "Smith J, Johnson A, Williams B" |
| 4 | **Year** | Publication year | "2023" |
| 5 | **Journal/Publication** | Journal name or publication venue | "Journal of Dermatological Research" |
| 6 | **DOI/PMID** | Digital Object Identifier or PubMed ID | "10.1000/example.2023.001" |
| 7 | **Study Type** | Type of research study | "Randomized Controlled Trial" |
| 8 | **Sample Size** | Number of participants | "120 patients" |
| 9 | **Intervention** | Treatment or intervention used | "Hydrogen-rich water (1.2 ppm)" |
| 10 | **Control** | Control group treatment | "Placebo water" |
| 11 | **Duration** | Study duration | "12 weeks" |
| 12 | **Outcome Measures** | What was measured | "Acne severity index, lesion count" |
| 13 | **Key Findings** | Main results and conclusions | "Significant reduction in acne lesions" |
| 14 | **Statistical Significance** | Statistical significance level | "p < 0.05" |
| 15 | **Limitations** | Study limitations | "Small sample size, short duration" |
| 16 | **Funding Source** | Who funded the study | "National Institutes of Health" |
| 17 | **Conflicts of Interest** | Any conflicts of interest | "None declared" |
| 18 | **Notes** | Additional notes or comments | "First study of hydrogen water for acne" |
| 19 | **Source URL** | URL where data was found | "https://hydrogenstudies.com/study/acne" |
| 20 | **Extracted Date** | When data was extracted | "2025-01-27T16:30:00.000Z" |
